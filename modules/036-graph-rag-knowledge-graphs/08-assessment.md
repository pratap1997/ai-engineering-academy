# 08 - Assessment

1. What is Graph RAG?
   - Answer: Combining Knowledge Graphs with LLM Retrieval Augmented Generation.
2. What are nodes in a Knowledge Graph?
   - Answer: Entities.
3. What is a community in a graph?
   - Answer: A densely connected cluster of nodes.
4. How does PageRank work?
   - Answer: By computing the stationary distribution of a random walk.
5. Why use Graph RAG over Vector RAG?
   - Answer: Better for multi-hop reasoning.
6. What is the Graph Laplacian?
   - Answer: D - A.
7. What is the Leiden algorithm used for?
   - Answer: Community detection.
8. What does local search do?
   - Answer: Traverses the neighborhood of specific starting entities.
9. What does global search do?
   - Answer: Summarizes across communities or the entire graph.
10. What is modularity?
    - Answer: A metric measuring the strength of division of a network into modules.
