# Module 002: References & Citations

## Primary Literature

1. **Minsky, M., & Papert, S. (1969)**. *Perceptrons: An Introduction to Computational Geometry*. MIT Press.
   - **Provenance**: Historical proof demonstrating single-layer perceptron limitations on non-linearly separable functions (XOR).
   - **Key Finding**: Multi-layer networks are required to compute predicate functions that are not order-1 linearly separable.

2. **Cybenko, G. (1989)**. *Approximation by Superpositions of a Sigmoidal Function*. Mathematics of Control, Signals and Systems, 2(4), 303-314.
   - **Provenance**: Universal Approximation Theorem proof.
   - **Key Finding**: A feedforward neural network with a single hidden layer containing a finite number of neurons with sigmoidal activation functions can approximate any continuous function on compact subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$ to arbitrary accuracy.

3. **Glorot, X., & Bengio, Y. (2010)**. *Understanding the difficulty of training deep feedforward neural networks*. AISTATS.
   - **Provenance**: Empirical analysis of Sigmoid vs Tanh activation functions and weight initialization.
