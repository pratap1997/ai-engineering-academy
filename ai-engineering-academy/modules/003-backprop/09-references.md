# Module 003: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., & Williams, R. J. (1986)**. *Learning representations by back-propagating errors*. Nature, 323(6088), 533-536.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing backpropagation algorithm to train multi-layer neural networks via gradient descent.

2. **Griewank, A., & Walther, A. (2008)**. *Evaluating derivatives: principles and techniques of algorithmic differentiation*. SIAM.
   - **Provenance**: Theoretical foundation of forward-mode and reverse-mode automatic differentiation.

3. **Karpathy, A. (2020)**. *micrograd: A tiny scalar-valued autograd engine*. GitHub repository.
   - **Provenance**: Inspiration for our educational scalar `Value` node implementation.
