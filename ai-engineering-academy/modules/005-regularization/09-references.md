# Module 005: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **Srivastava, N., Hinton, G., Krizhevsky, A., Sutskever, I., & Salakhutdinov, R. (2014)**. *Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting*. Journal of Machine Learning Research, 15(56), 1929-1958.
   - **Provenance**: Seminal paper introducing Dropout, inverted scaling, and ensemble interpretation.

2. **Ioffe, S., & Szegedy, C. (2015)**. *Batch Normalization: Accelerating Deep Network Training by Reducing Internal Covariate Shift*. ICML.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing Batch Normalization, running statistics, and scale/shift parameters ($\gamma, \beta$).

3. **Ba, J. L., Kiros, J. R., & Hinton, G. E. (2016)**. *Layer Normalization*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1607.06450.
   - **Provenance**: Paper introducing Layer Normalization for sequence models and Transformers.
