# Module 007: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S., & Sun, J. (2015)**. *Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1512.03385.
   - **Provenance**: Landmark CVPR 2016 paper introducing ResNet, residual learning, skip connections, and Bottleneck blocks.

2. **He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S., & Sun, J. (2016)**. *Identity Mappings in Deep Residual Networks*. European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV).
   - **Provenance**: Paper proving mathematical properties of clean identity shortcuts and pre-activation residual units.
