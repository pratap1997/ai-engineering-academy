# Module 009: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **Hochreiter, S., & Schmidhuber, J. (1997)**. *Long Short-Term Memory*. Neural Computation, 9(8), 1735-1780.
   - **Provenance**: Seminal paper introducing LSTM, the Cell State, Constant Error Carousel (CEC), and Gated memory units.

2. **Cho, K., van Merriënboer, B., Gulcehre, C., Bahdanau, D., Bougares, F., Schwenk, H., & Bengio, Y. (2014)**. *Learning Phrase Representations using RNN Encoder-Decoder for Statistical Machine Translation*. Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP).
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) and Reset/Update gates.
