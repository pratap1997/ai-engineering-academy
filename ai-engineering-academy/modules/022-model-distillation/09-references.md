# Module 022: References

1. **Hinton, G., Vinyals, O., & Dean, J. (2015)**. *Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network*. NIPS Deep Learning Workshop.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper establishing Knowledge Distillation and temperature scaling.

2. **Sanh, V., Debut, L., Chaumond, J., & Wolf, T. (2019)**. *DistilBERT, a distilled version of BERT: smaller, faster, cheaper and lighter*. arXiv:1910.01108.
   - **Provenance**: Production implementation extracting 6-layer student from 12-layer BERT.

3. **DeepSeek-AI (2025)**. *DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning*. arXiv:2501.12948.
   - **Provenance**: Sequence-level distillation of 800,000 reasoning trajectories into Qwen 1.5B/7B/14B/70B student models.
