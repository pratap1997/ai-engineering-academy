## 2. DistilBERT Architecture & Pruning

DistilBERT (Sanh et al., 2019) extracts a 6-layer student from 12-layer BERT-Base:
- **Initialization**: Student layers are initialized by taking **every second layer** ($1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11$) of BERT-Base.
- **Triple Loss**: Distillation loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{KL}}$) + Masked LM loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{MLM}}$) + Cosine embedding hidden-state loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{cos}}$).
- **Result**: $40\%$ parameter reduction, $60\%$ speedup, $97\%$ test performance retained.

## 3. Sequence-Level Speculative Distillation

In modern LLMs:
- **Token-Level Distillation**: Student learns Softmax logit distribution per token step.
- **Sequence-Level Distillation**: Student is trained on synthetic text completions generated by the Teacher (Used in DeepSeek-R1 Distill series).
