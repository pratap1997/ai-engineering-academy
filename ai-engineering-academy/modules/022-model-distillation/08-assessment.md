# Module 022: Assessment & Readiness Check

## 1. Formative Questions

### Q1: Why is temperature scaling $T > 1$ essential during knowledge distillation?
**Answer**: At standard temperature $T=1$, softmax probabilities for non-target classes are compressed close to zero ($10^{-5}$ or smaller), hiding structural relationships. Raising temperature $T$ softens the logit distribution, exposing "dark knowledge"—the relative probability ratios among incorrect classes—which guides the Student model's optimization landscape.

### Q2: Why is the KL divergence loss scaled by $T^2$ in Hinton's distillation formula?
**Answer**: The gradient of softmax with temperature $T$ scales down by $\frac{1}{T^2}$. Multiplying the soft KL divergence loss by $T^2$ ensures that the gradient magnitude contributed by soft teacher targets remains balanced with the hard cross-entropy loss regardless of the temperature choice.

### Q3: What is the difference between Token-Level and Sequence-Level Distillation?
**Answer**: Token-level distillation transfers full logit probability distributions per token step ($T^2 D_{\text{KL}}$). Sequence-level distillation trains the student model on complete synthetic text sequences generated by the Teacher model (e.g. DeepSeek-R1 distilling 800,000 reasoning paths into Qwen 1.5B/7B).
