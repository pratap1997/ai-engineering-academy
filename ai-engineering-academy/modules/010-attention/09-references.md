# Module 010: References & Citations

1. **Vaswani, A., Shazeer, N., Parmar, N., Uszkoreit, J., Jones, L., Gomez, A. N., Kaiser, Ł., & Polosukhin, I. (2017)**. *Attention Is All You Need*. NeurIPS.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing Scaled Dot-Product Attention, Multi-Head Attention, and the full Transformer architecture.

2. **Bahdanau, D., Cho, K., & Bengio, Y. (2015)**. *Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate*. ICLR.
   - **Provenance**: Pioneering paper introducing the original additive (Bahdanau) attention mechanism for Seq2Seq translation.

3. **Devlin, J., Chang, M. W., Lee, K., & Toutanova, K. (2019)**. *BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding*. NAACL.
   - **Provenance**: BERT demonstrates Bidirectional Self-Attention for language understanding pre-training.
