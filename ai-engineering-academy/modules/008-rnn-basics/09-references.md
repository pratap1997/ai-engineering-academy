# Module 008: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **Elman, J. L. (1990)**. *Finding Structure in Time*. Cognitive Science, 14(2), 179-211.
   - **Provenance**: Seminal paper introducing the Elman Recurrent Neural Network architecture and hidden state recurrence.

2. **Pascanu, R., Mikolov, T., & Bengio, Y. (2013)**. *On the difficulty of training Recurrent Neural Networks*. ICML.
   - **Provenance**: Landmark paper identifying the mathematical conditions for vanishing/exploding gradients in RNNs and proposing Gradient Norm Clipping.

3. **Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., & Williams, R. J. (1986)**. *Learning representations by back-propagating errors*. Nature, 323(6088), 533-536.
   - **Provenance**: Original paper establishing Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT).
