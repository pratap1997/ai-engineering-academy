# Module 004: References & Citations

## Primary Literature & Provenance

1. **Kingma, D. P., & Ba, J. (2014)**. *Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1412.6980.
   - **Provenance**: Seminal paper introducing Adam optimizer, exponentially weighted moment estimation, and bias correction derivation.

2. **Loshchilov, I., & Hutter, F. (2017)**. *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW)*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1711.05101.
   - **Provenance**: Paper proving L2 regularization failure in adaptive gradient algorithms and proposing AdamW.

3. **Sutskever, I., Martens, J., Dahl, G., & Hinton, G. (2013)**. *On the importance of initialization and momentum in deep learning*. ICML.
   - **Provenance**: Proof of classical momentum and Nesterov accelerated gradient benefits in deep architectures.
