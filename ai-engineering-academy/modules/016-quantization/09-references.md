# Module 016: References

1. **Dettmers, T., Lewis, M., Belkada, Y., & Zettlemoyer, L. (2022)**. *LLM.int8(): 8-bit Matrix Multiplication for Transformers at Scale*. NeurIPS.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces 8-bit vector-wise quantization with outlier isolation for Transformers.

2. **Frantar, E., Ashkboos, S., Hoefler, T., & Alistarh, D. (2022)**. *GPTQ: Accurate Post-Training Quantization for Generative Pre-trained Transformers*. arXiv:2210.17323.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces second-order Taylor optimization for 4-bit weight-only quantization.

3. **Lin, J. et al. (2023)**. *AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization for LLM Compression and Acceleration*. arXiv:2306.09782.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces activation-aware salient weight protection for INT4 LLMs.

4. **Xiao, G. et al. (2023)**. *SmoothQuant: Accurate and Efficient Post-Training Quantization for Large Language Models*. ICML.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces activation-to-weight difficulty migration for W8A8 quantization.
