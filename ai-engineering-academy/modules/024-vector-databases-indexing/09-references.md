# Module 024: References

1. **Malkov, Y. A., & Yashunin, D. A. (2018)**. *Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Using Hierarchical Navigable Small World Graphs*. IEEE TPAMI.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing HNSW graph indexing algorithm.

2. **Jegou, H., Douze, M., & Schmid, C. (2011)**. *Product Quantization for Nearest Neighbor Search*. IEEE TPAMI.
   - **Provenance**: Establishes Product Quantization (PQ) and IVF-PQ vector compression techniques.

3. **Cormack, G. V., Clarke, C. L., & Buettcher, S. (2009)**. *Reciprocal Rank Fusion Outperforms Condorcet and Individual Rank Learning Methods*. SIGIR.
   - **Provenance**: Mathematical foundation for Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) in hybrid search.
