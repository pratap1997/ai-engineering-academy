# Module 019: References

1. **Hu, E. J., Shen, Y., Wallis, P., Allen-Zhu, Z., Li, Y., Wang, S., Wang, L., & Chen, W. (2021)**. *LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models*. ICLR.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing low-rank matrix decomposition $\Delta W = (alpha / r) B A$ for LLM fine-tuning.

2. **Dettmers, T., Pagnoni, A., Holtzman, A., & Zettlemoyer, L. (2023)**. *QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs*. NeurIPS.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces NF4 4-bit quantization, double quantization, and Paged Optimizers for low-memory LLM tuning.

3. **Aghajanyan, A., Gupta, A., & Zettlemoyer, L. (2020)**. *Intrinsic Dimensionality Explains the Effectiveness of Language Model Fine-Tuning*. ACL.
   - **Provenance**: Empirical proof that pre-trained language models have very low intrinsic dimension.
