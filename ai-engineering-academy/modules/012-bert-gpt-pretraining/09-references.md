# Module 012: References

1. **Devlin, J., Chang, M.-W., Lee, K., & Toutanova, K. (2019)**. *BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding*. NAACL-HLT.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces Masked Language Model (MLM), Next Sentence Prediction (NSP), and the BERT embedding scheme (Token + Segment + Position).

2. **Radford, A., Narasimhan, K., Salimans, T., & Sutskever, I. (2018)**. *Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training* (GPT-1). OpenAI.
   - **Provenance**: First demonstration of large-scale CLM pre-training for transfer learning across NLP tasks.

3. **Radford, A., Wu, J., Child, R., Luan, D., Amodei, D., & Sutskever, I. (2019)**. *Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners* (GPT-2). OpenAI.
   - **Provenance**: Demonstrates zero-shot task transfer purely from CLM pre-training at 1.5B parameters.

4. **Liu, Y. et al. (2019)**. *RoBERTa: A Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach*. arXiv:1907.11692.
   - **Provenance**: Shows that removing NSP, using more data and dynamic masking significantly improves BERT representations.
