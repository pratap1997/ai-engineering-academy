# Module 021: References

1. **Rafailov, R., Sharma, A., Mitchell, E., Ermon, S., Manning, C. D., & Finn, C. (2023)**. *Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model*. NeurIPS.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing DPO algorithm and implicit reward formulation.

2. **Ouyang, L. et al. (2022)**. *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback*. NeurIPS (InstructGPT).
   - **Provenance**: Original PPO RLHF pipeline baseline.

3. **Ethayarajh, K. et al. (2024)**. *KTO: Model Alignment as Prospect Theoretic Optimization*. arXiv:2402.01306.
   - **Provenance**: KTO extension for unpaired binary feedback data.
