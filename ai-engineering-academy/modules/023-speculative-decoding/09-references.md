# Module 023: References

1. **Leviathan, Y., Kalman, M., & Matias, Y. (2023)**. *Fast Inference from Large Language Models via Speculative Decoding*. ICML.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing Speculative Decoding and rejection sampling distribution recovery proof.

2. **Chen, C. et al. (2023)**. *Accelerating Large Language Model Decoding with Speculative Sampling*. arXiv:2302.01318.
   - **Provenance**: Concurrent paper establishing speculative sampling speedup bounds.

3. **Cai, Z. et al. (2024)**. *Medusa: Simple LLM Inference Acceleration with Multiple Decoding Heads*. arXiv:2401.10774.
   - **Provenance**: Draft-free speculative decoding architecture using multiple head predictions.
