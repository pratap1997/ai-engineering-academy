# Module 014: References

1. **Su, J., Lu, Y., Pan, S., Murtadha, A., Wen, B., & Liu, Y. (2021)**. *RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding*. arXiv:2104.09864.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing RoPE, now standard in LLaMA, Mistral, PaLM, and Qwen.

2. **Press, O., Smith, N. A., & Lewis, M. (2022)**. *Train Short, Test Long: Attention with Linear Biases Enables Input Length Extrapolation* (ALiBi). ICLR.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces ALiBi distance penalties for extreme context extrapolation.

3. **Shaw, P., Uszkoreit, J., & Vaswani, A. (2018)**. *Self-Attention with Relative Position Representations*. NAACL-HLT.
   - **Provenance**: First paper to introduce relative position representations into Transformer self-attention.

4. **Raffel, C. et al. (2020)**. *Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer* (T5). JMLR.
   - **Provenance**: Introduces logarithmic relative position bucketing used in T5.
