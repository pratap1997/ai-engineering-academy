# Module 025: References

1. **Nogueira, R., & Cho, K. (2019)**. *Passage Re-ranking with BERT*. arXiv preprint arXiv:1901.04085.
   - **Provenance**: Foundational paper introducing BERT-based Cross-Encoder re-ranking for information retrieval.

2. **Gao, Y., et al. (2023)**. *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2312.10997.
   - **Provenance**: Comprehensive survey detailing Naive RAG, Advanced RAG (Pre-retrieval, Post-retrieval re-ranking), and Modular RAG.

3. **Karpukhin, V., et al. (2020)**. *Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering (DPR)*. EMNLP 2020.
   - **Provenance**: Establishes Dual-Encoder (Bi-Encoder) dense vector passage retrieval for open-domain QA.
