## How to update this log

After completing each pass, update the row:

```
| Pass | Date (ISO) | Evidence (link to commit, PR, or video) | What changed in understanding | Any misconception that was corrected |
```

A misconception entry looks like:
```
Believed: bias scales the input values.
Corrected: bias shifts the decision boundary independently of inputs.
Implication: would have misdiagnosed boundary positioning problems.
```
